Privacy Policy

Plain language, no surprises. Last updated 14 November 2026.

The short version

  • Your text goes to Claude to get an answer. We never store your text or the answers.
  • With your own Anthropic key, requests go straight from your Mac to Anthropic. They never touch our servers.
  • We keep an account only if you sign in: your email, your plan and your credit balance.
  • You can delete your account in the app, and we erase what is linked to you.
  • Sharing a run with us to improve the product is always your choice, asked one run at a time, and you can edit it first.

Who we are

Per My Last is a Mac app that helps you reply, understand and write with Claude. You can reach us at hello@permylast.app about anything on this page.

Your text and Claude's answers

With your own Anthropic API key, the app sends your request from your Mac directly to Anthropic. Our servers are not involved.

With Per My Last credits, the request goes through our server, which adds our API key and forwards it to Anthropic. We pass the answer back and do not save the text you sent or the answer. Our log of each run records only: which prompt template and model ran, token counts, how long it took, whether it succeeded, and a short error reason if it failed.

Anthropic processes your request under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not send anything through the app that you would not send to Claude.

The app reads the text you selected or copied only when you press the shortcut or choose it, never in the background. Screenshots you attach are sent to Claude with that request and nowhere else.

What we store about your account

Only if you sign in (needed for credits, a plan or a Lifetime licence):

  • Sign-in details: your email address, and your name if Apple or Google provides it, plus the ID that Apple or Google gives us for you. If you sign in with Apple, we keep the token we need to revoke it when you delete your account.
  • Plan and credits: your plan, your monthly credit balance and how many you have used.
  • Purchases: the App Store transaction details Apple sends us (product, dates, status). We never see your card or payment details.
  • Free credits protection: a one-way hash of a random ID kept on your Mac, so one Mac cannot claim the free credits many times. The hash is kept after account deletion for that reason only.

What stays on your Mac

Your Anthropic API key is kept in your Mac's Keychain. Your settings, presets and prompts are stored locally in the app. None of this is sent to us.

Anonymous usage stats (you can turn them off)

The app can send anonymous first-run events, such as which setup step was reached, with a one-way hash of a random install ID and the app version. It never includes your text, email, key or anything you selected. Turn it off in Settings → General → Share anonymous setup stats.

Ratings and sharing a run

After you have used the app for a while, it occasionally asks how a run went (Bad, Fine or Good). You can switch the question off in Settings → General → Ask me how results went, or press “Don't ask again”.

  • Your rating is sent anonymously with settings only: model, effort, mode, preset, app and macOS version, app language and whether the run used your own key. It follows the “Share anonymous setup stats” switch.
  • Sharing the run is a separate, optional step. Only if you press “Share this run” do we receive the prompt, the text you gave and the result. Before that, the app shows you exactly what would be sent as a log, and you can edit or remove anything in it. Screenshots are never sent.

Shared runs are stored with a one-way hash of a random install ID, not with your name, email, account or API key. Because nothing links a shared run to you, we cannot find it later to delete it. If it might contain private details, edit them out or choose “No thanks”. We use shared runs only to improve the prompts, models and quality of Per My Last.

This website

This website uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to count visits and see how pages are used (clicks, scrolling, and anonymised session recordings), which set cookies in your browser. On English pages, your browser also asks ipapi.co for your approximate country so we can offer the Vietnamese version; we do not store the result. The account page signs you in with a one-time code sent to your email.

Who else handles data

  • Anthropic processes requests sent to Claude.
  • Cloudflare hosts our servers and database.
  • Apple handles purchases and Sign in with Apple. Google handles Sign in with Google.
  • Resend delivers sign-in emails.
  • Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity serve the website analytics above.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to your account in Settings and choose to delete it. We then erase your account, sign-in sessions, profile, plan, credit usage and run history. The only thing that stays is the one-way install hash described above. Anonymous ratings and shared runs cannot be linked back to you, so they cannot be removed. You can also ask us at hello@permylast.app.

Children

Per My Last is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app. Questions: hello@permylast.app.
