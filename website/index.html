How it works

Three steps, no app switching

It sits in your menu bar and stays out of the way until you need it.

01

Copy some text

An email, a Slack message, a paragraph you're not happy with — anywhere on your Mac.

02

Press the hotkey

A small window appears near your cursor with the text already loaded in.

03

Pick a prompt, copy the result

Rewrite, summarize, fix grammar, reply to an email — then paste it back where it belongs.

Capabilities

Built for the message you're stuck on

Not another chat window. A tool that meets you inside the app you're already writing in.

Global hotkey

Cmd+Shift+S by default, works from any app — Mail, Slack, Notes, your browser.

Email-aware replies

Paste an email in and ask for a reply — Per My Last recognizes the intent and drafts one.

Six ready-made prompts

Rewrite, Summarize, Explain, Fix Grammar, Professional, Reply Email — or write your own.

Your key, your model

Bring your own Anthropic API key. Choose Sonnet, Opus, Haiku, or Fable. No account, no subscription.

Privacy
No server, no accounts Requests go straight from your Mac to Anthropic's API. We don't run a backend that sees your text.
Nothing stored but settings Your API key and preferences stay in macOS's local app storage, encrypted at rest by the OS.
Open about what it does It reads your clipboard when you press the hotkey, and nothing else, nothing in the background.
